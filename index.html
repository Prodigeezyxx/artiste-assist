Straight answers

The questions every first call starts with.

Do we still need a promoter?

Yes. Promoters like Cokobar sell the rooms and own the local market. We run the machine behind the show: routing, permits, freight, crew and the money between everyone. Nobody replaces anybody.

Which markets do you cover?

The UK and Canada today, with local partners on the ground in each city. If we cannot run your market well, we will tell you on the first call instead of learning on your tour.

What does it cost?

A retainer per run, quoted after we scope the routing. We do not take a cut of your ticket sales. You see every cost line before anything is booked.

How early should we come to you?

Ideally a season before the run. Visas and freight reward patience. If the timeline is tight we will say what is still possible and what has to wait.

Planning a tour?
Send us the markets.

Tell us the cities and the rough dates. You get back a route, a timeline and a number, usually within a week.
